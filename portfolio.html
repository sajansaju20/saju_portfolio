MY STORY

I’m a Computer Science Engineering student specializing in Cyber Security, exploring the dynamic intersection of cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and autonomous threat defense.

I’m particularly interested in building systems that don’t just react to threats, but understand, investigate, and predict them. From fine-tuning local LLM guardrails against prompt injection to developing offline, privacy-first Retrieval-Augmented Generation architectures with Qwen2.5, my work focuses on building robust defense mechanisms.
